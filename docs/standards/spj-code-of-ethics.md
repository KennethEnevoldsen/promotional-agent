## Structure of the code

The code has four principles, each with a list of things journalists should do:

1. **Seek truth and report it.** Be accurate and fair; verify; give context; identify
   sources; correct errors.
2. **Minimize harm.** Treat sources, subjects and the public as human beings deserving of
   respect.
3. **Act independently.** Serve the public; avoid conflicts of interest; refuse favours.
4. **Be accountable and transparent.** Explain choices to the audience; acknowledge and
   correct mistakes promptly and prominently.

## Clauses we rely on (quoted verbatim, from "Seek Truth and Report It")

> Ethical journalism should be accurate and fair. Journalists should be honest and
> courageous in gathering, reporting and interpreting information.

> Take responsibility for the accuracy of their work. Verify information before
> releasing it. Use original sources whenever possible.

> Remember that neither speed nor format excuses inaccuracy.

> Provide context. Take special care not to misrepresent or oversimplify in promoting,
> previewing or summarizing a story.

> Gather, update and correct information throughout the life of a news story.

## How they map to this repo

| Clause | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Verify information before releasing it; use original sources | Recompute every number from the results repo (`AGENTS.md`, rule 1) |
| Neither speed nor format excuses inaccuracy | The 300-character limit is no excuse for dropping a claim's scope |
| Not misrepresent or oversimplify in promoting or summarizing | A post and its card title *are* the summary; they must not overstate |
| Update and correct throughout the life of a story | When results are rerun or a claim stops holding, say so in a follow-up post |

## A note on versions

Copies of the 1996 version that circulate online name headlines, teasers, promotional
material and graphics explicitly in this clause. The 2014 revision folded that into the
"Provide context" clause above. We have not verified the 1996 wording against an
authoritative source, so quote the 2014 text.
